MelPump gives you a clear view of how your heat pump really performs, and this app brings that insight into Homey. Follow temperatures, COP and energy use in real time, see the heat pump's consumption in Homey Energy, and get notified when MelPump detects a problem such as short cycling or a drop in efficiency.

Combine your heat pump with the rest of your home: block hot water when electricity is expensive, force solar mode when your panels produce a surplus, switch on holiday mode when everyone leaves, or adjust the heat curve ahead of cheap hours. To get started, create an API token in your MelPump account settings and add your heat pump in Homey. This is an unofficial community app: it is not developed, supported or endorsed by MelPump, so please report problems with the app to the app developer and not to MelPump support.
